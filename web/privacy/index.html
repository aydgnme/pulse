Pulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Pulse does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No data leaves your device. Your best score is saved locally on your phone and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • No account is required or offered.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs are included.
  • No network access is used by the game.

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or lose. If this policy ever changes, this document will be updated before any change takes effect.
